Legislative Work

GT Colorado State Student Board & Education Advocacy

Through my work with the GT Colorado State Student Board and the Boulder Valley School District GT Youth Council, I have focused on improving access to advanced learning opportunities, especially in STEM for students at my school. After my freshman year, I recognized that the existing Gifted and Talented Support Program at my school did not fully meet student needs. For example, students needed to update their Advanced Learning Plans but the program lacked the resources to help students do so. So l initiated the creation of a stronger school-level GT program that aligned better with student needs.

I worked with my school Principal and my school’s GT coordinator to build a framework where students can share their needs, access mentorship, and pursue advanced academic and passion-based opportunities. This experience showed me how student voice, administrative collaboration, and policy advocacy can work together to create meaningful change.

Advocacy Priorities

  • Expanding access to advanced STEM coursework and research pathways
  • Creating stronger student voice in gifted education decisions
  • Supporting school-level GT programming and mentorship
  • Connecting policy conversations to real student experiences
